Try asking

Ask a policy question as Ana, a nurse. Pick a suggestion on the left or type your own.

Every answer cites the passage it came from. Drafts, outdated versions and documents Ana isn't allowed to read are filtered out before search, so they can't leak into an answer. When nothing she can read answers the question, the assistant says so.

Evaluation: guardrails on vs off
