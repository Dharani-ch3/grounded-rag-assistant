# Paid Time Off Policy

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO covers vacation and personal days.

## Carryover
Employees may carry over up to 10 unused PTO days into the next calendar year.

## Requesting time off
Submit PTO requests by email to your manager at least 1 week in advance.
