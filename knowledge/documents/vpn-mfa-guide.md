# VPN and Multi-Factor Authentication Guide

## Setting up multi-factor authentication
Install the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone and scan the QR code shown at first sign-in. Text message codes are not allowed for staff accounts.

## Connecting to the VPN
Use the GlobalProtect VPN client to reach internal systems from outside the clinic network. The VPN disconnects after 12 hours and you must sign in again.

## Lost phone or locked account
If you lose your phone or are locked out, call the IT service desk at extension 4357. The service desk verifies your identity before resetting multi-factor authentication.
