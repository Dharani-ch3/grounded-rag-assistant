# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Travel meals
While traveling for work, meals are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Receipts
Itemized receipts are required for any expense over $25.

## Deadlines
Submit expense reports in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense. Reports submitted after 60 days require approval from Finance.

## Mileage
Personal car use for work travel is reimbursed at $0.65 per mile. Commuting between home and your usual work site is not reimbursable.
