# Paid Time Off Policy

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO covers vacation and personal days. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## Carryover
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any unused days above 5 expire on December 31.

## Requesting time off
Submit PTO requests in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance. Your manager approves or declines the request within 3 business days. Requests during the December holiday period are approved in the order they are received.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO. Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year, and a doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.
