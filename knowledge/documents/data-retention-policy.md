# Data Retention Policy

## Patient records
Patient medical records are retained for 10 years after the patient's last visit. Records for minors are retained until the patient turns 28.

## Email
Business email is retained for 3 years and then deleted automatically, unless it is under a legal hold.

## Audit logs
System audit logs are retained for 6 years.

## Legal holds
When a legal hold is issued, the Compliance Officer notifies the affected teams, and no covered data may be deleted until the hold is released.
