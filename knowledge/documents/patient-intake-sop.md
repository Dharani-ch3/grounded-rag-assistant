# Patient Intake Procedure

## Identity check
Confirm every patient's identity with two identifiers, full name and date of birth, before any care is given. Never use the room number as an identifier.

## Allergies and medications
Review allergies and current medications at every visit, even if the patient was seen recently. Record "no known allergies" explicitly rather than leaving the field blank.

## Vital signs
Take and record vital signs within 15 minutes of rooming the patient. Repeat blood pressure after 5 minutes if the first reading is above 140/90.

## Language access
Offer a qualified medical interpreter to any patient who prefers a language other than English. Family members should not interpret clinical information.
