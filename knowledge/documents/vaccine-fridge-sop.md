# Vaccine Refrigerator Monitoring

## Temperature range
Vaccine refrigerators must stay between 2°C and 8°C. Check and log the temperature twice a day, at opening and at closing.

## Out-of-range readings
If a reading is outside 2°C to 8°C, label the vaccines "do not use", keep them refrigerated, and notify the pharmacy within 1 hour. Do not discard vaccines until the pharmacy confirms they are no longer viable.

## Power outages
During a power outage, keep the refrigerator door closed and move vaccines to the backup unit if the outage lasts longer than 2 hours.
